Plumber at Voorhees Town Center, NJ

Voorhees Town Center is the former Echelon Mall site, now home to retail, offices and the township's municipal complex, with a townhome redevelopment approved. This page is for business owners, tenants and building managers. Homeowners nearby should start with Echelon.

What Voorhees Town Center is

Voorhees Town Center is a commercial and mixed-use district in the western part of Voorhees, inside the Echelon area. It occupies the former Echelon Mall, and the township's municipal building opened there in 2011.

As of 2017 the center had about 730,000 square feet of retail space with Boscov's as its only anchor. We do not have current tenant counts, so this page does not describe who is open today. The office building Four Echelon Plaza, across from the Town Center, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2026.

Why this matters for plumbing

Large mixed-use projects bring new water, sewer and fire-line connections, and nearby businesses may notice work in the street. Those are general effects of building projects, not predictions for this one.

When a project reaches the permit stage, plumbing permits go through the Voorhees Construction Department, listed at 2400 Voorhees Town Center. Ask the department what applies to your work before it starts.

Who handles what in a commercial space

Leases differ. Use this as a starting point for a conversation, not as a legal answer.

Often the tenant

Inside the suite you rent.

  • Fixtures such as sinks, toilets and faucets
  • Waste and grease from your own operation
  • Reporting a leak the day you see it

Often the building owner

Shared parts of the building.

  • Shared mains and risers
  • Roof and common-area drains
  • A water heater that serves several units

Before you book commercial plumbing work

  • ✓Read the lease for who pays for plumbing repairs

  • ✓Ask for a written scope of work and a price before work begins
  • ✓Plan the visit around your business hours

Services that fit shops, offices and restaurants

Day-to-day work is covered under commercial plumbing. A drain that keeps backing up in a kitchen or restroom may need drain cleaning, and where grease builds up in a line, hydro jetting clears it more thoroughly than a cable.

New fixtures, a tenant fit-out or an added restroom fall under plumbing installation, with permits planned first. Residents of the surrounding neighborhood can read about plumbing in Echelon.
