Emergency Faucet Repair in Voorhees, NJ

A drip is a nuisance. A valve that will not close, a tub spout leaking into the wall or a supply line spraying under a vanity is an emergency, and we answer the 24/7 line for it in Voorhees, NJ.

Find the shutoff under the sink or at the water heater and the main by the meter, and close the nearest one before you call.

Call (855) 575-2890

Before anything else, find the shutoff

Faucet repair is the replacement or rebuild of the internal parts of a tap, tub spout, shower valve or hose bib: cartridges, washers, O-rings, seats and the stop valves that feed them. Most of those parts sit behind a handle you can see.

If water is spraying or a stop valve will not turn, close the whole-house main and call. Forcing a seized valve can snap it.

The type decides the fix, so it helps to know what you have.

Hard water is the quiet culprit

According to New Jersey American Water's published report, the Delaware system that serves Voorhees measures hardness at 88 mg/L, or 5.1 grains per gallon. That is enough to crust aerators, stiffen cartridges and scratch ceramic discs over time.

A faucet that sputters or sprays sideways usually has a clogged aerator before it has a failed valve. Unscrew the aerator, soak it in vinegar and see whether the stream straightens.

Urgent: act first

Leaking through the wall or ceiling right now

A shower valve, tub spout or burst supply connector that leaks inside a wall can stain ceilings below within hours. This is the situation where waiting turns a repair into drywall, insulation and mold.

Tell us on the call where you see the water, whether the leak slows when the fixture is off, and whether anyone can reach the main.

  • 1Close the fixture's own stop valve if it has one, or the main at the meter or where the line enters
  • 2Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and take pressure off the leak
  • 3Put a bucket under drips and move anything valuable out of the path of water
  • 4Take photos of stains and call (855) 575-2890 so a plumber can plan access

Do not stand under a sagging ceiling.

Repair the faucet or replace it

Repair

Best when the body is sound.

  • Dripping spout, stiff handle or weak flow
  • Parts are still available for the make and model
  • Finish is intact

Replace

Best when the cost of parts approaches a new unit.

  • Pitted or corroded body
  • Obsolete model with no cartridge
  • Leak from the body or base casting
  • You want a different style or a pull-down spout

Replacing a faucet with no change to the piping is ordinary maintenance under NJ DCA guidance, though plumbing work should be done by licensed persons.

Quick diagnosis from your side

You can narrow the cause in a few minutes.

  • ✓Drip from the spoutWorn seal or cartridge. Shutting the stop valve stops it.
  • ✓Drip below the handleFailing O-ring or packing nut.
  • ✓Water under the sinkLoose supply nut, cracked supply line or a leaking drain slip joint. Dry the area and watch.
  • ✓Only hot or only cold flowsClogged cartridge port, stuck check valve or debris.
  • ✓Low flow from one faucetAerator scale, usually. If all faucets are weak, the problem is upstream. See water line repair.

What affects the price of a faucet or valve repair

Cartridge availability

Common parts are quick. Imported or discontinued styles may take longer to source.

In-wall access

Tub and shower valves need access panels or tile work.

Shutoff valves that fail

If the stop valve will not close, replacing it comes first.

Water damage

A leak inside a wall can add drying and repairs.

Fixture quality

Solid brass bodies cost more and last longer than thin castings.

Insurance note. Homeowner insurance may cover water damage from a sudden burst connector but typically not the worn faucet itself, so check your policy. A clear price is given before work starts, so the choice between repair and a new faucet is yours.

Winter and outdoor spigots

A hose bib left connected to a hose in freezing weather can split behind the wall. The leak starts when the thaw comes, which is why a spigot that drips inside the house after winter needs a look.

A frost-free bib extends the valve seat into the heated space, and that is a simple replacement for outdoor faucets that have failed more than once. If a pipe has already split, see burst pipe repair.
