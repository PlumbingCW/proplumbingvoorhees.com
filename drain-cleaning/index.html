Emergency Drain Cleaning in Voorhees, NJ

Water coming up through a floor drain or out at the baseboards is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for it. While you wait, stop running water in the house, move anything valuable off the floor and keep children and pets out of the wet area.

Drain cleaning means removing the blockage from a fixture drain or a main line with a cable machine, a hand auger or a water jet so wastewater flows away again. We also find out why it clogged, because a line that stops twice in a month has a cause.

Floor drain overflowing or water behind the toilet right now

When a basement floor drain bubbles up or water seeps out along the baseboards, the blockage is usually in the main line, not in one fixture. Every toilet, tub and sink drains into that same pipe, so anything you flush or pour adds to the problem.

A tub that fills with gray water when the washer drains is the same warning in a smaller form.

  • 1Stop adding waterNo flushing, no laundry, no dishwasher, no showers until the line is open.
  • 2Protect what you canLift boxes and electronics off the floor and keep your feet out of standing water near outlets.
  • 3Skip the chemical drain openerIt sits in a full pipe, burns skin and makes the next plumber's job less safe.
  • 4Call the 24/7 lineTell us which drains are affected. If only one fixture is slow, we can plan a regular visit instead.

Sewage in the house carries bacteria, so wear rubber boots and gloves if you must walk through it, and wash well afterward.

Cable, auger or water jet: which tool fits

The right tool depends on what is blocking the pipe and how far away it is.

Hand auger

Short, fast and gentle on fixtures.

  • Good for tubs, lavatories and toilets
  • Pulls out hair and wipes
  • Not for lines past the foundation

Drum cable machine

A motor-driven cable with a cutting head.

  • Reaches the main line from a cleanout
  • Chops through roots and soft blockages
  • Leaves a thin film on pipe walls

Hydro jet

High-pressure water that scours the pipe wall.

  • Removes grease and scale a cable only pokes through
  • Needs a clear path and a sound pipe
  • See hydro jetting for when it makes sense

The honest trade-off: a cable is quicker and cheaper to run, but a jet leaves the pipe cleaner. If a line has been cabled twice already, the second visit should start with a camera.

What drives the price of a drain call

We give a clear price before work starts. These are the things that move it.

Fixture drain or main line

A tub drain is a short job. A main line from a cleanout takes longer and needs bigger equipment.

Access

A cleanout you can reach is quick. A line with no cleanout may need a toilet pulled or a roof vent opened.

Cause of the clog

Hair and soap differ from roots and grease, and roots call for a cutting head.

Time of day

After-hours and weekend calls take priority dispatch, which affects what a visit costs.

Follow-up work

A camera inspection or a jetting pass is separate from the first clearing.

Insurance note. Homeowner insurance may cover water damage from a sudden sewer backup only if you added that endorsement, so check your policy before you file. When you compare quotes, ask whether the price covers the camera look and a return visit if the clog comes back.

Try this before you call, and when not to

For a single slow fixture, these steps are safe and often enough.

  • ✓Remove the stopper and pull out hair with a plastic drain tool
  • ✓Use a plunger with a good seal, with the overflow opening blocked on a tub
  • ✓Pour hot (not boiling) water down a kitchen line to move soft grease
  • ✓Stop if more than one fixture is affected, because the main line is likely involved
  • ✓Never mix cleaners, and never use a chemical opener on a fully blocked pipe

If water rises in a floor drain, the DIY window has closed. A rented machine can break a pipe joint you cannot see.

When the drain keeps coming back

A clog that returns in weeks points to a damaged or bellied section, roots entering a joint or an old line with scale on its walls. Repeating the same cabling only delays the real answer.

At that point we recommend a camera look through sewer inspection, and if the pipe is sound, a pass of sewer line cleaning on the main. The same repeat-clog pattern turns up in older lines around Somerdale and Lindenwold.
