What counts as a plumbing emergency in Voorhees

A plumbing emergency is any problem that is damaging the building right now or is unsafe to live with. This table sorts common situations so you can decide quickly whether to call an emergency plumber tonight.

What to do before the plumber arrives

The minutes before a plumber arrives decide how much damage you end up with. Most of what helps is free and takes a minute or two.

Do these in order, and skip any step that puts you near standing water around electrical outlets or appliances.

  • 1Close the main water shutoffIt is usually near the water meter or where the line enters the house. Turn it fully clockwise.
  • 2Kill power near the waterIf water is reaching outlets, appliances or a panel, switch off the breaker from a dry spot. If you cannot reach it safely, stay out and say so on the phone.
  • 3Open a faucet at the lowest levelThis drains the pipes and relieves pressure so less water escapes.
  • 4Move valuables and lift rugsClear the floor around the leak and set towels or a bucket under drips.
  • 5Take photos, then callPhotos of the damage help with an insurance claim. Call the emergency line and tell us what you found.

If you smell gas at any point, stop. Leave the building, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not turn lights or switches on or off on the way out.

Voorhees housing, pipes and water

How to choose a plumber you can trust

Plumbing work in New Jersey must be done by licensed persons. A few questions before anyone starts work protect you, whether you call us or someone else.

  • ✓Ask for the licenseA real plumbing contractor can tell you the license they work under. Verify it with the state before work begins.
  • ✓Ask for the price before work startsA written or spoken price for the job, with the main cost drivers explained, lets you compare quotes.
  • ✓Ask what the repair will actually involveYou should hear the cause, the fix and what else might turn up once walls or floors are open.
  • ✓Ask about permitsLike-for-like water heater replacement needs notice to the enforcing agency. Other jobs vary, so ask the Voorhees Construction Department.
  • ✓Be wary of pressureA plumber who insists you decide in the next minute for non-urgent work is a warning sign.

A plumber called at night should still answer all five. Urgency is not a reason to skip the price or the license conversation.

When not to wait for morning

Active water you cannot stop, sewage in the house, no heat in freezing weather and any smell of gas are all reasons to call now. A dripping tap or a slow drain is not, and a scheduled visit costs less than an after-hours dispatch.
