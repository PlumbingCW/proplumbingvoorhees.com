About Voorhees Pro Plumbing in Voorhees, NJ

Voorhees Pro Plumbing is a plumbing service line for homes and businesses in Voorhees, New Jersey, and the towns around it. You call one number, describe the problem, and a licensed and insured plumber is arranged for you.

What this business is

We are a local service line, not a national call center. Our address is 700 Haddonfield-Berlin Road in Voorhees, NJ 08043, in Camden County. Calls to the number on this site are about plumbing in Voorhees and the neighboring towns of Gibbsboro, Somerdale, Lindenwold and West Berlin.

The work itself is done by licensed and insured plumbing professionals. We keep the claims on this site small on purpose. You will not find invented review scores, job totals or founding stories here, because a plumber's word on the phone and the work in your home should carry the weight instead.

Our job is to make the first step easy. Instead of searching through ads at 2 a.m. with water on the floor, you reach a person who asks the right questions and gets a plumber moving.

How a call goes

Most callers have never hired an emergency plumber before, so here is the whole path from dial tone to finished repair.

  1. 01

    You describe what you see

    Tell us what is happening: where the water is, what it sounds like, whether it is hot or cold, and when it started. You do not need plumbing words. Plain description works best.

  2. 02

    We ask a few short questions

    We ask about your address, whether you can reach the shutoff valve, and whether anyone smells gas or sees water near electrical outlets. These answers decide how urgent the visit is.

  3. 03

    You get safety steps while you wait

    If water is spreading, we talk you through closing the main valve and keeping people away from wet electrical areas. If gas is involved, we tell you to leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

  4. 04

    A plumber is dispatched or scheduled

    Urgent problems go out as soon as a plumber is available, and we offer same-day appointments when available for less urgent work. We do not promise an arrival time we cannot control.

  5. 05

    You hear the price before work starts

    The plumber looks at the problem, explains the options, and gives you a clear price before work starts. You decide. If you say no, the conversation ends there.

What the service covers

We handle the everyday and the urgent. That includes drain cleaning for slow or backed-up lines, water heater repair when the hot water stops, and leak detection when a bill climbs and nobody can see why. For a pipe that has already let go, we send help for burst pipe repair.

Larger jobs are covered too, such as sewer line repair, sump pump work for wet basements, and repiping for aging supply lines. Businesses can ask about commercial plumbing as well.

The full list lives on the plumbing services page. If your problem is not listed, call anyway. A short conversation will tell you whether we can help.

What we will not do

We will not quote a price over the phone for a problem nobody has seen. Pipes hide things, and a number given blind is usually wrong in one direction or the other. We will also tell you when a repair is not worth doing, for example when a very old fixture or heater is better replaced than patched.

How to verify any plumber's license

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by licensed persons. That rule comes from the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs, which oversees plumbing licensing in the state. It applies to us and to every other company you might call.

  • ✓Ask for the license holder's name and numberA real plumber gives this without hesitation. Write it down before anyone enters your home.
  • ✓Check it with the stateSearch for the New Jersey Division of Consumer Affairs license verification service and look the license up yourself. Do not rely on a number printed on a flyer or truck alone.
  • ✓Match the name to the person at your doorThe license should belong to the plumber, or to the contractor who employs them. If the answers do not line up, pause the job.
  • ✓Ask about insuranceA properly insured company can tell you so directly. Damage to a ceiling or floor is exactly when you want that answered in advance.
  • ✓Get the price in plain terms firstBe cautious of anyone who will not explain the price before starting. A clear price before work starts is a normal request.

We encourage you to run this check on us as well. A plumber who is confident in their license is glad to be checked.
