Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Voorhees, NJ

If sewage is coming up into a basement bathroom or the floor drain, call our 24/7 line now and keep every faucet and toilet off. Open a window if the smell is strong, and stay out of standing water because it carries bacteria.

Sewer line repair means fixing or replacing the pipe that carries waste from your house to the municipal sewer, whether the damage is a crack, a root-split joint, a sag or a full collapse. The fix depends on what a camera shows, not on a guess.

How sewer lines fail

Old clay and cast iron crack at joints. Orangeburg (a tar-paper fiber pipe used decades ago) softens and ovals. Roots find a hairline gap, enter for moisture and widen it until the line holds water instead of moving it.

A bellied section (a low spot where the pipe has settled) holds sewage and solids, so the line clogs at the same spot again and again. That is the failure a cable cannot cure.

Repair options and their trade-offs

Three approaches cover most failures. A camera tells us which one fits.

Spot repair

Dig down and replace one short section.

  • Good for a single broken joint
  • Leaves the rest of the old line in place
  • Requires a trench at that spot

Trenchless lining

A resin-soaked liner is cured inside the old pipe.

  • Limited digging at access points
  • Works on cracks and root gaps
  • Does not fix a collapsed or badly sagging pipe

Full replacement

New pipe from the house to the connection.

  • Best when the pipe is failing along its length
  • Longest job and most disruption
  • Brings a clean, uniform line

Homeowners often ask us about the trenchless option that inflates a liner. It is a good fit for many lines, but not for pipe that has already collapsed or lost its slope.

Who owns what in Voorhees

The township collects sewage and conveys it to the Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority system. Voorhees code Chapter 51 includes a section on sewer maintenance and replacement responsibility, so ask the township which part of the line, from the house to the connection, belongs to you.

Permit rules for sewer work vary, so ask the Voorhees Construction Department before you commit to a method. If your property is on a septic system instead of the sewer, the Camden County Department of Health is part of that picture.

What drives the cost of sewer repair

Length of the damaged section

A single joint is a small job. A full house-to-street run is large.

Depth and surroundings

Deep pipe under a driveway, patio or mature tree costs more to reach.

Method chosen

Lining, spot repair and replacement each need different crews and materials.

Restoration

Patching a lawn or paving is separate from the pipe work.

Permits and inspections

Ask the township what applies to the work before pricing it.

Insurance note. Homeowner insurance may not cover pipe that failed from age or roots, but it may cover resulting interior damage if you have sewer backup coverage, so check your policy. When shopping around for estimates, ask each company to base its quote on a recorded camera run, not a guess from the surface.

When a repair is the wrong move

If the camera shows a short, isolated crack, a full replacement is more than you need. If it shows a long run of broken joints, repeated spot repairs add up and a replacement is usually the better value. We will tell you which side your line falls on.

A recorded sewer inspection is the first step for any repair estimate. When roots are the only problem and the pipe is sound, sewer line cleaning may be all you need.
