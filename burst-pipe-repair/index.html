Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Voorhees, NJ

A burst pipe can put gallons of water into a house every minute, so close the main shutoff first, then call our 24/7 line at (855) 575-2890. Switch off the breakers for the wet area only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Burst pipe repair means stopping the flow, cutting out the split or cracked section and joining new pipe in its place, then restoring pressure and checking every connection. The goal is a dry house tonight and a pipe that will not fail at the same spot.

Urgent: act first

Water pouring from a wall or ceiling right now

Every minute counts, and the steps below are the same whether the pipe is in the attic, the wall or the crawlspace. Do them in order, and call as soon as the water is stopped or if you cannot stop it.

  • 1Close the main shutoffTurn the valve clockwise where the water line enters the house, usually near the meter or the water heater.
  • 2Open the lowest faucetDrain what is left in the pipes so less water escapes at the break.
  • 3Kill power in the wet zoneSwitch off the breaker for that room. Do not touch switches with wet hands or while standing in water.
  • 4Turn off the water heaterIf the pipe feeds it, shut off the heater so it does not run dry and overheat.
  • 5Call and documentPhotograph the damage, then call the 24/7 line. We offer same-day scheduling when available.

If water has reached an outlet, the ceiling is bowed or you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility from outside.

Cold snaps and old mains around Voorhees

Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces and garages are the usual freeze breaks in Voorhees. Ask your township or utility for frost guidance, and treat any pipe that has frozen once as a pipe that may split at the next thaw.

A water main break and a house pipe break look the same at first. How bad is the break

Drip

A pinhole or a weeping joint

Close the valve to that fixture, place a bucket and book a repair soon.

Spray

A steady spray or a bulging wall

Shut the main, drain the line and call the same day.

Flood

Shut the main, cut power in the area and call the 24/7 line at once.

Why pipes burst, by material

PipeHow it failsTypical repair
CopperFreeze splits along the seam, or pinholes from corrosionCut out the section and sweat in new copper
Galvanized steelRust closes the pipe from inside, then it cracks at fittingsReplace the section, often more of the run
CPVC and PEXCracks at fittings, or splits from freezingReplace with push fittings or new pipe
Cast iron drainCracks and leaks, rarely bursts from pressureSee sewer line repair
Supply valve or stopWorn stem breaks when turnedReplace the valve

Patch, section replacement or repipe

A burst pipe is also a message about the pipe around it.

Patch the break

Fast and low disruption.

  • Fine for one freeze split in good pipe
  • Leaves old pipe in place
  • Does not help if corrosion is widespread

Replace the section

Cut out a run and install new pipe.

  • Removes the weak stretch
  • Needs wall access
  • Piping may be replaced to repair a leak as ordinary maintenance in New Jersey

Repipe the house

Replace most or all supply lines.

  • Fits galvanized or repeated failures
  • Largest job and disruption
  • Like-for-like replacement is minor work with notice to the enforcing agency

If you are shopping for estimates, ask each company which of these three they are recommending and why. A good answer refers to what the pipe looks like, not just the quote.

What affects the cost of burst pipe repair

Location of the break

A basement pipe in the open is simple. A pipe inside a finished wall or ceiling takes more work.

Pipe material

Copper, PEX and galvanized each call for different parts and methods.

Amount of pipe replaced

A short splice is quick, and a long section adds up.

Time of the call

After-hours emergency dispatch is priced differently from a booked visit.

Drywall and finish

Patching and painting after the repair are separate trades.

Insurance note. Homeowner insurance may cover sudden burst pipe damage but typically not the pipe itself or damage from a long-term leak, so check your policy and document everything. Ask for a clear price before work starts, and ask whether the quote covers testing and drywall opening.

Preventing the next one

Insulate exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, disconnect outdoor hoses before cold weather and keep the heat on when you travel. If you find the break was caused by corrosion, a camera or a leak detection visit can show whether other sections are close to failing.
