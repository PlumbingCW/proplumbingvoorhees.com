Plumbing Installation in Voorhees, NJ

Plumbing installation is adding or relocating fixtures, supply lines, drains and appliance connections, so that water arrives where you want it and waste leaves cleanly. It covers a replacement toilet, a basement bathroom, a water softener and a hose bib on the garage wall.

Planning matters more than speed here. A cheap install that ignores slope, venting or permits becomes a repair job later. Urgent leaks can still use our 24/7 line.

Start with the drain, not the fixture

Every fixture needs a path for waste: a trap to hold a water seal, a vent to let air in, and a drain sloped to the main. A new bathroom fails or succeeds on those three things before anyone chooses a faucet.

If your drain is below the sewer line or far from the stack, a sewage ejector or a macerating unit may be needed. We check the location and the existing stack before we price anything.

Rough-in now or fixtures only

If walls are open, the cheapest time to add plumbing is now.

Rough-in during a remodel

Pipes go in with the walls open.

  • Easiest time to move fixtures
  • Easy to add stubs for a future bath
  • Inspection happens before drywall

Fixtures only

Existing lines stay where they are.

  • Quicker and lower cost
  • Limited to the current layout
  • May still need new shutoffs

A frequent regret is closing a wall before adding a stub for a future bathroom or an outdoor line. Ask about it.

Softeners, filters and hard water

New Jersey American Water's published report lists hardness for the Delaware system at 88 mg/L, about 5.1 grains per gallon. That is on the harder side, so softeners and filters are common installation requests in Voorhees.

A softener needs a drain for regeneration, an overflow path, a bypass and a power outlet. If you already have one that leaks at the valve or overflows, we also service the plumbing connections.

Gas hookups are not a weekend project

Connecting a range, dryer or grill to a gas line means pipe, shutoff, pressure test and leak check. If you ever smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you do anything else.

Switching an appliance from natural gas to propane involves different orifices and pressure, and the work belongs to licensed persons. Ask the Voorhees Construction Department what applies to your job.

Permits and digging rules in Voorhees

Voorhees permits run through the Construction Department at 2400 Voorhees Town Center, and plumbing must be done by licensed persons. New Jersey's plumbing subcode follows the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code. Replacing a fixture with no piping change is ordinary maintenance, while new fixtures and layout changes usually need a closer look, so ask the office.

For anything outdoors, call 811 at least three business days before you dig, and hand dig within two feet of marked lines. New dwellings need a sewer connection permit from the Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority. Neighbors in Somerdale and West Berlin deal with their own offices.
