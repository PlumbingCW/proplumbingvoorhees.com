Tankless Water Heater in Voorhees, NJ

A tankless water heater heats water only when a tap opens, passing it through a gas burner or electric element and a heat exchanger instead of storing it in a tank. In Voorhees, two things decide how well one lives: the hardness of the water, and the size of the gas line feeding it.

Is tankless right for your house?

It is a good fit for some households and a poor one for others.

Choose tankless if

You want endless hot water and the space back.

  • Large family with back-to-back showers
  • Wall space but no room for a tank
  • A gas line that can be upsized

Think twice if

The house has special limits.

  • Gas meter or line is already at capacity
  • Water is hard and you will not flush it
  • You need hot water during a power outage

A tank is the simpler choice for small, steady households. Our water heater installation page compares the types side by side.

Hard water and tankless: what 88 mg/L means

According to New Jersey American Water's water quality report, the Delaware system that serves Voorhees shows hardness of 88 mg/L, about 5.1 grains per gallon. Heated water drops calcium and magnesium onto the narrow passages of a heat exchanger.

A tankless unit has far less water volume than a tank, so even a thin layer of scale cuts flow and triggers error codes. This is why a yearly flush is part of owning one here, not an optional extra.

What to do before we arrive

  • ✓Smell gas? Leave first. Get everyone out of the building and call your gas utility or 911 before calling a plumber.
  • ✓Write down the error code. Most units show a numbered code on the display, and the code narrows the fault quickly.
  • ✓Shut the unit off. Turn off its power switch or breaker. If it is leaking, close the cold water inlet valve under or beside the unit.
  • ✓Do not open the gas side. Leave the gas valve and burner compartment to a licensed plumber.

How a tankless flush works

A proper descale needs a pump and a bucket, not a garden hose.

  1. 01

    Shut off gas, power and water

    The unit is isolated using its service valves. These valves are often missing on older installs, which we add.

  2. 02

    Connect hoses and a small pump

    Hoses attach to the hot and cold service ports and a pump circulates a descaling solution through a bucket.

  3. 03

    Circulate for the recommended time

    Scale dissolves and the fluid turns cloudy. Time depends on the model and how scaled it is.

  4. 04

    Rinse and clean the inlet filter

    Fresh water flushes out the solution. The small screen on the cold inlet is cleaned or replaced.

  5. 05

    Test flow and error codes

    We run hot water at several taps, check temperature rise and confirm the unit fires cleanly.

If you have never had yours serviced, the first flush often pulls out more scale than later ones.

Gas sizing is the step most quotes skip

A tank burner is small and runs for a long time. A tankless burner is large and runs in short, hard bursts. Many houses built with a tank in mind have a gas line sized for that smaller appliance.

Before an install, the pipe diameter and length from the meter, plus every other gas appliance on the line, have to be checked. The gas utility may need to upsize the meter. Skipping this step is the usual reason a new unit cuts out when the dryer starts.

Venting is the other half. Tankless units need approved stainless or plastic vent pipe, and the distance to windows and air intakes matters.

When NOT to go tankless

Skip it if the gas line cannot be upsized at a reasonable cost, or if you are unlikely to keep up with flushing. A neglected tankless unit in hard water can fail earlier than a tank.

What affects tankless costs

Unit size and brand

Higher flow models cost more and need more gas.

Gas line and meter upgrade

Larger piping or a utility meter change adds to the total.

Venting method

Direct vent and wall penetrations take more labor than a simple replacement.

Service valves and filters

Isolation valves for flushing are worth adding at install.

Flush complexity

A heavily scaled heat exchanger takes more time than a lightly scaled one.

Insurance note. Maintenance is not usually covered by homeowner insurance, though sudden water damage from a failed unit may be, so check your policy. Ask for a clear price before work starts and what the manufacturer's warranty requires for yearly service.

Replacing a tank with a tankless unit

If you are moving from a tank, the old unit and any old boiler can be removed and hauled away. Plumbing work must be done by licensed persons in New Jersey, and the Voorhees Construction Department is the office to ask about permits for a change of heater type.
