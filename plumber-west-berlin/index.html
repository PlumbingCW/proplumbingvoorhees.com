Sewer backup in West Berlin: what to do first

Sewage in a tub, a toilet that gurgles when you run the washer or a floor drain that fills up all point to a blocked line. Treat it as an emergency, because the water that comes back up is not clean.

  • 1Stop using waterNo flushing, laundry or dishwasher. Every gallon you send down has nowhere to go.
  • 2Look at the whole houseIf every drain is slow, the problem is likely the lateral or the main. If only one is, it is probably a branch.
  • 3Keep people and pets awayDo not wade through it, and skip chemical drain cleaners, which can splash back.
  • 4Call the 24/7 lineA camera inspection tells us whether the blockage is yours or the township's.

If neighbors on your street have the same backup, mention it when you call. That points toward the main.

Wet yards and road drainage near Marion, Myrtle, Lester and Haddon Avenues

The Camden County hazard mitigation plan (draft) says roadway flooding is a major concern across the township. It names Marion and Myrtle Avenues, Lester Avenue, and Haddon Avenue between Bate and Lucas Avenues as drainage priorities. This is runoff pooling in low spots, not a creek spilling over.

For a homeowner, that means rain can leave a yard or crawl space wet even without river flooding. A sump pump moves that water out, but it does not cure a street that cannot drain. Before you route a discharge line, ask the township what it allows and call 811 if you plan to dig.

If your basement stays damp after every storm, basement waterproofing and better downspout runs often help more than a bigger pump.

How a lateral problem is found and fixed

The usual order of work once a plumber arrives.

  1. 01

    Camera inspection

    A small camera on a flexible cable runs through the cleanout. It shows roots, a belly, a crack or a clog, and tells us how far from the house it sits.

  2. 02

    Clear the line

    We use a cable machine for soft blockages or hydro jetting for grease, scale and roots.

  3. 03

    Locate and mark

    A locator finds the pipe from above, and 811 marks underground utilities before any digging.

  4. 04

    Repair the damaged section

    A spot repair or a full run is chosen from the footage. Our sewer line repair page explains the options.

Cable, jet or dig: choosing a fix

Each method has a place. Here is when each one fits.

Cable machine

Fast for a soft clog.

  • Good for paper and light buildup
  • Leaves some residue on pipe walls
  • Weak against roots

Hydro jetting

High-pressure water scours the pipe.

  • Cleans grease and scale
  • Cuts light roots
  • Not for a collapsed or cracked pipe

Dig and repair

For a broken or sagging line.

  • Fixes the cause
  • Disturbs the yard
  • Needs 811 marking first

A camera run before any method keeps you from paying to clear a line that actually needs repair.

West Berlin sewer and township facts

Berlin Township Public Works maintains and operates the sanitary sewer system and its pumping stations. The township does not maintain the lateral, so a backup in your own line is a call to a licensed plumber. The Municipal Building sits on Route 73 South if you need to visit the township.

This page is for the township and ZIP 08091 West Berlin. It is not Berlin Borough, which has ZIP 08009 and is a separate town with its own offices.

Water heaters and fixtures: ask your water provider

We do not assume which utility supplies your home, so check your bill or ask your water provider about hardness and service line material. Hard water leaves scale in tanks and on aerators, and both shorten life. If you see white crust or hear popping, that is sediment.

A like-for-like water heater replacement is minor work under the New Jersey construction code, and the enforcing agency must be notified. Plumbing must be done by licensed persons. Ask the township which office handles the notice for your address.

For hidden dampness or a high bill, leak detection finds the source before anyone opens a wall.
