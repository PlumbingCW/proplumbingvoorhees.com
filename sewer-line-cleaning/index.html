Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Voorhees, NJ

When every drain in the house runs slow together, or water shows up in the basement, the main line is blocked and that is a call for our 24/7 line. Do not run the washer or flush while you wait, because every gallon you add has nowhere to go but your floor.

Sewer line cleaning is the clearing of the main drain that carries all of a home's wastewater to the sewer connection, using a heavy cable, a jet or both. It restores flow through the part of the system that every fixture shares.

Urgent: act first

Backup in the basement: what to do first

A main line stoppage sends wastewater to the lowest opening, which is usually a floor drain, a basement toilet or a laundry sink. It can happen at any hour, and overnight is common because evening laundry and dishes fill the line.

  • 1Turn off water-using appliances and stop flushing
  • 2Keep electronics and boxes off the floor and avoid wet outlets
  • 3Do not pour chemical drain opener into a pipe that is full of water
  • 4Call the 24/7 line and say whether the water is rising or holding steady
  • 5Open the cleanout cap only if you know where it is, and stand to the side when you do

After-hours dispatch is available, and same-day scheduling applies when a crew is free.

What happens to the water after the cleanout

In Voorhees the township collects sewage and conveys it to the Camden County Municipal Utilities Authority system. Your main line is the private end of that chain, and what you flush becomes part of a public line downstream.

That is why wipes sold as flushable, paper towels and kitchen grease are poor choices. They tangle with roots in your pipe, and the same mix is a headache for the system beyond your property.

How we clear a blocked main

  1. 01

    Locate the cleanout

    A two-way cleanout near the foundation lets us reach both directions along the line.

  2. 02

    Run the cable

    A drum machine with a cutting head breaks up roots, paper and grease until the line opens.

  3. 03

    Follow up with a flush

    Water at volume carries loosened material out and shows whether flow is back.

  4. 04

    Recommend the next step

    A camera look follows if the blockage was roots or came back recently.

A decision rule for repeat blockages

One stoppage in several years is a maintenance event. Two in one year is a sign. Three means the pipe has a defect that cleaning alone cannot fix, and the honest answer is a camera run followed by repair or a planned jetting schedule.

What shapes the price of sewer line cleaning

Cleanout or no cleanout

A good cleanout speeds the work. A pulled toilet takes more time.

What is in the pipe

Paper and grease clear quickly. Dense root mass takes more passes.

Length of the run

A long line to a far connection needs more cable and time.

Tool needed

A cable is one level of effort, and a jet is another.

Hour and day

Night and weekend calls are dispatched on priority, and priced to match.

Insurance note. Homeowner insurance may cover cleanup after a sudden backup only with a sewer backup endorsement, so check your policy before you call the carrier. Ask any company you call whether a camera look afterward is included or priced separately.

Keep the main line open

  • ✓Put only toilet paper and human waste down the toilet
  • ✓Collect cooking grease in a can and throw it away with the trash
  • ✓Run a kitchen sink strainer to catch food scraps
  • ✓Know where your cleanout is and keep it uncovered
  • ✓Have the line cleaned or looked at when a tree grows over it
  • ✓Do not plant willows or maples directly above the line

These habits do not fix a cracked pipe, but they cut the number of blockages in a healthy one.

Beyond cleaning

If the cable keeps pulling out roots, sewer inspection shows where they enter. A scaled but sound pipe often responds well to hydro jetting, while a failing one needs sewer line repair.
