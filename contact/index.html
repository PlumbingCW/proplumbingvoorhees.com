Call or email: which to use

Call when water is moving, a drain is backing up, the hot water has stopped, or you smell something wrong. A live conversation lets us give safety steps while a plumber is arranged. Email suits questions that can wait, such as asking whether we cover a service or planning a project for next week.

Email replies are not instant, so please do not use email for an active leak. We have no form on the site, which means nothing you type here is collected. The address is plain text: contact@proplumbingvoorhees.com.

Urgent: act first

If water is flooding right now

Do these things before or while you dial. They limit damage in the minutes before help arrives.

  • 1Close the main shutoffTurn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. In many Voorhees homes it is near the water meter or where the line enters the basement.
  • 2Stay clear of electricityIf water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, do not walk through it. Turn off power only if you can reach the breaker while standing on dry ground.
  • 3Open a low faucetDraining the lines at the lowest tap relieves pressure and reduces what keeps leaking.
  • 4Call the 24/7 lineReach us at (855) 575-2890 and tell us what you did. We will take it from there.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before calling us.

What to have ready when you call

You do not need to prepare much. A few facts help us decide how urgent the visit is and what the plumber should bring.

  • ✓Your addressInclude the unit or building number if you live in an apartment or townhome.
  • ✓The symptomsTell us what you see, hear and smell. Gurgling drains, a hot water tank that clicks, a ceiling stain or a sudden drop in pressure are all useful clues.
  • ✓Where the shutoff isTell us whether you found the main valve and whether it turned. A stuck valve changes the plan.
  • ✓When it startedA leak that began this minute differs from one that has been creeping for weeks.
  • ✓A call-back numberUse a phone that can take a call or text while you are dealing with the problem.

You do not need to send photos. Describing the problem in your own words is enough.
