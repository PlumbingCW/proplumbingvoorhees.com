Where Echelon is

Echelon is an unincorporated community and census-designated place inside Voorhees Township, Camden County. It sits in the western part of the township, between Cherry Hill and Gibbsboro, and covers about 2.8 square miles. Voorhees Town Center, the former Echelon Mall, is in Echelon.

Echelon is not a separate town. It has no municipal government of its own, so permits and local rules come from Voorhees Township. The Census boundary exists for counting people, so neighbors may describe the edges of Echelon differently than a map does.

The neighboring borough has its own page: see our Gibbsboro plumbing page. For shops and offices around the Town Center, see Voorhees Town Center.

Water heaters, drains and leaks

Three jobs are worth knowing about whatever your housing type. A water heater that stops heating or leaks at the base needs a quick look, because a small drip can turn into a wet floor.

A slow drain in one fixture is usually a local clog. When several fixtures back up together, the cause is further down the line, and drain cleaning with a camera look is the sensible next step.

For a stain or a damp spot with no obvious source, leak detection finds the pipe before anyone cuts into a wall. None of this is specific to Echelon, and we do not claim these problems are more common here than elsewhere.

Businesses near the Town Center

Echelon includes a commercial center, and shops, offices and restaurants have a different set of questions from a household. Landlord and tenant duties, shared mains and work hours all matter. Our commercial plumbing page covers the services, and the Town Center page covers the area.
