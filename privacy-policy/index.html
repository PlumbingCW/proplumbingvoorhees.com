Privacy Policy

This policy explains in plain English how Voorhees Pro Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our line.

Last updated: October 2026.

Call (855) 575-2890

The short version

This website has no forms and does not ask visitors to submit information through the site. If you want to reach us, you call or send an email yourself.

We keep this policy short and readable. It covers what the site does, what happens when you call, and how to contact us with questions.

What this website collects

The site is a set of informational pages. It does not have a contact form, a booking form, a login or a payment page. Nothing you type on the site is sent to us, because there is nowhere to type.

The site itself does not use cookies. Your browser and the outside services described below may handle their own technical data, which we do not control.

Third-party services the site loads

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts so that text looks consistent. To do this, your browser requests the font files from Google, which means Google can see standard connection details such as your IP address.

Some pages also include an embedded Google Map showing our location. Loading the map likewise connects your browser to Google. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive it.

  • Google Fonts, used to display page text
  • Embedded Google Map, used to show where we are located

What happens when you call

When you call our line, we may learn your phone number and the details you choose to tell us, such as your address and a description of the problem. Your phone carrier also shows us the number you are calling from.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the person who answers and ask how else you can reach us.

How we use call details

We use phone numbers and call details to answer your request, arrange a plumber, and follow up on the job. We also use them to review how calls are handled so we can improve the service.

We ask only for what is needed to help you, such as your location and the nature of the problem. You can decline to answer any question, though that may limit what we can do.

  • To understand your plumbing problem and arrange a visit
  • To confirm the appointment and address
  • To check the quality of how calls are handled
  • To train the people who answer the phone

Sharing information

To send a plumber to you, we pass along the details they need to do the job, such as your address and the problem you described. We share only what is needed for that purpose.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it. This policy does not make claims about other situations that we cannot back up, so if you have a specific concern, please ask us.

Email

If you email us at contact@proplumbingvoorhees.com, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it to reply to you. Please avoid sending sensitive information by email, such as financial account details.

Your choices

You can browse the site without giving us anything. You can block cookies and third-party content in your browser settings if you prefer, though some fonts or the map may then not display.

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing us. We will respond as best we can. If you live somewhere with specific privacy rights, you may have additional rights under your local law.

Children

This site is meant for homeowners and businesses, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. The current version is always the one on this page.

Contact us

For privacy questions, email contact@proplumbingvoorhees.com. Our address is 700 Haddonfield-Berlin Road, Voorhees, NJ 08043.
